Professor, School of Mathematics and Statistics, Rochester Institute of Technology

Mathematical modeling of the fate and transport of plastic pollution, hydrodynamics, and data assimilation

I build mathematical models to understand where plastic pollution goes in lakes and oceans, and develop data-assimilation methods that blend those models with observations. The same tools reach into lake and ocean hydrodynamics, hyperspectral remote sensing, and the electrical dynamics of the heart.

Research

01

Plastic pollution: fate and transport

Particle-transport models driven by lake and ocean currents estimate where plastic goes, from the first full budget for the Great Lakes to global marine reservoirs.

Plastic pollution research
02

Data assimilation

Ensemble methods that blend numerical models with observations, applied to lakes and estuaries, the Martian atmosphere, and the electrical dynamics of the heart.

Data assimilation research
03

Remote sensing

Hyperspectral imaging to detect and track vehicles from the air, including the open AeroRIT benchmark now used across the imaging community.

Selected publications

  1. 2026
    How size-dependent settling can both bias and inform microplastics observations
    M. J. Hoffman and M. H. DiBenedetto. Environmental Research Communications

    Shows how particle settling skews microplastic sampling, and how that bias can be turned into information.

  2. 2021
    Global simulations of marine plastic transport show plastic trapping in coastal zones
    Onink, Jongedijk, Hoffman, van Sebille, and Laufkötter. Environmental Research Letters

    Most floating plastic stays trapped near coastlines rather than drifting to open-ocean garbage patches.

  3. 2020
    Modeling the three-dimensional transport and distribution of multiple microplastic polymer types in Lake Erie
    Daily and Hoffman. Marine Pollution Bulletin

    The first mass estimates of plastic throughout the water column and on the bottom of any of the Great Lakes.

  4. 2020
    AeroRIT: A new scene for hyperspectral image analysis
    Rangnekar, Mokashi, Ientilucci, Kanan, and Hoffman. IEEE Transactions on Geoscience and Remote Sensing

    An openly released benchmark dataset now used across the hyperspectral imaging community.

  5. 2017
    Inventory and transport of plastic debris in the Laurentian Great Lakes
    Hoffman and Hittinger. Marine Pollution Bulletin

    The first full-system plastic budget for the Great Lakes, estimating nearly 10,000 metric tons entering the lakes each year.
